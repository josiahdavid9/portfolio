    I turn complicated briefs into clear, accessible design.

    I'm David, a designer based in Redcar. Since 2021 I've been the sole designer and artworker for three community initiatives, creating everything from a coffee shop's identity and signage to accessible print resources for refugees and vulnerable households.

    Before that I founded Indigo English, an independent digital brand whose visual toolkits were featured on Miro. I came to design through project leadership and teaching, so I start with who the audience is and what they need to do next, then let the layout do the work.

    Core competencies

    • Brand identity and logo design
    • Print: posters, leaflets, menus and guides
    • Signage and environmental graphics
    • Web design with no-code platforms
    • Brand guidelines and asset libraries
    • Accessible, inclusive communication

    Tools: Adobe Creative Cloud, Affinity, Squarespace, WordPress, Notion, Miro

    Selected organisations: Redcar Baptist Church, Indigo English

    Design philosophy

    • Start with the audience.Who is this for, and what do they need to do next? The layout follows from that answer.
    • Clarity is a kindness.For people reading in a second language or under stress, a clear hierarchy matters more than decoration.
    • Build the system, then keep it tidy.Consistent guidelines and organised asset libraries let a small team stay on brand.
