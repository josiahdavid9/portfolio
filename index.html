I'm David, a designer and digital content creator based in Redcar. My route in was unusual: a politics degree, then years teaching English and building online learning, before design became the day job. That background shapes how I work: start with who the audience is and what they need to do next.

Since 2021 I've been Creative Project Lead at Redcar Baptist Church, the sole designer behind Common Ground, the Welcome Project and Warm & Well. I also volunteer as creative director at East Cleveland Baptist Church, producing identity and live stream graphics.
